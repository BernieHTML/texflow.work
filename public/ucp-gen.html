How it works

        

          

            
1

            
Paste a URL

            
Any public product page — Shopify, WooCommerce, custom sites. We read Open Graph, JSON-LD and meta tags.

          

          

            
2

            
Get UCP JSON

            
We extract title, description, price range, images, variants and tags into the Universal Commerce Protocol format.

          

          

            
3

            
Negotiate or save

            
Copy the JSON, open it directly in a Gander room, or save it to the TexFlow /.well-known agent catalog.

          

          

            
4

            
Agent-readable

            
Saved products appear at /.well-known/ucp-products.json — discoverable by any UCP-aware agent.
