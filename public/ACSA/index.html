Mission Control / Australia

ACSA

Australian Crewed Spaceflight Agency

Australia's first crewed spacecraft attempt.

ACSA is an Australian aerospace effort aiming to develop the primitive one-person Aurora capsule and send the first astronaut to space from Australian soil within a decade.

  • Partnering with One Giant Leap Australia.
  • Built by aspiring astronauts and space builders across Australia.
  • Focused on spaceflight, aviation, physics, rocketry, and public inspiration.
