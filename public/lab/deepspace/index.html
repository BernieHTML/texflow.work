🛰️ The two faintest whispers

Voyager 1 & 2 launched in 1977 and are now the most distant human-made objects, over 20 billion km away in interstellar space. Their signals reach Earth at about 10-16 watts — a few hundred billion times weaker than a digital watch battery — which is why it takes a 70-metre dish to hear them. When the DSN is listening to a Voyager, it lights up orange above.
