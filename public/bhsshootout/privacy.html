Privacy Policy

BHS Penalty Shootout · last updated 27 August 2026

BHS Penalty Shootout is a small game made for students at Blaxland High School. It is run by an individual, not a company, and it is not an official school project. This page says plainly what it stores about you and what it does not.

What we collect

  • Your email address — used only to identify your account when you sign in.
  • Your username — shown to opponents and to friends. Choose one you are happy for other players to see.
  • Your password — never stored as you typed it. It is put through a one-way hash (scrypt) with a random salt, so nobody, including us, can read it back.
  • A sign-in token — a random string kept in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • Your game record — crowns, shootouts played and won, your chosen squad, and who you are friends with.

What we do not collect

  • No real names, no photos, no location, no contacts, no device identifiers.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone else. There is nobody to share it with.

Where it lives

Everything is stored in plain files on a single server at texflow.work, in Australia. It is not backed up to any third-party service. Matches in progress are deleted automatically once they go idle.

Security, honestly stated

This is an early version of a hobby game. Passwords are hashed and traffic is encrypted in transit, but the app has not been through a security review. Please do not reuse a password you use anywhere else, and do not put anything sensitive in your username.

Students and parents

Some players will be under 18. The only thing the game asks for is an email address, a username and a password — nothing that identifies a student to a stranger unless they choose a username that does. If you are a parent or guardian and want an account and everything attached to it deleted, ask and it will be, no questions.

Deleting your account

Email bernie.dyke@gmail.com from the address on the account, or send the username. The account, its crowns and its friend links are removed permanently.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the new version appears here.
