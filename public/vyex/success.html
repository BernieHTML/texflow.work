How to add this expert to Vy

Follow these steps to add the expert to the user's Vy account so they can use it in workflows.

  1. Copy the preamble for this expert (from the URL above or by opening the preamble URL and copying the content above the instruction block).
  2. Open Vy and go to Experts.
  3. Click Create.
  4. Fill out the expert name and other fields based on the preamble content.
  5. Paste the copied preamble into the Knowledge base field.
  6. Click Create expert.
