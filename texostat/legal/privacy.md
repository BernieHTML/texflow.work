# TexoStat Privacy Policy

**Governing law:** Australian Privacy Act 1988  
**Last updated:** 21 April 2026

---

## 1. Who We Are

TexoStat is a data feed relay service operated by TexFlow (texflow.work). This policy explains what personal data we hold, how we use it, and your rights under the Australian Privacy Act 1988.

## 2. Data We Hold

### What we store

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| TEX user ID (username) | Identifying your subscriptions and channel ownership | Until account deletion |
| Subscription list | Routing your SSE feed | Until you unsubscribe or delete |
| Channel ownership records | Allowing you to publish and manage channels | Until channel deletion |
| Connection logs (IP address, timestamp, TEX user ID) | Security, debugging, rate limiting | 30 days |

### What we do NOT store

- Your email address or password (held by TEX, not duplicated here)
- Content of SSE streams you receive
- Agent decision logs or outputs
- Personal financial information

## 3. Shared Authentication

TexoStat imports your identity from TEX via shared authentication. No additional personal data is collected at sign-up because there is no separate TexoStat sign-up. TEX's privacy policy governs the storage of your email and credentials.

## 4. Third-Party APIs

TexoStat fetches data on your behalf from third-party APIs (CoinGecko, USGS, NASA, etc.). Your identity is not shared with those APIs. Requests are made server-side by TexoStat's poller, not from your device.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

- As required by Australian law or a valid court order
- To protect the safety of users or the public

## 6. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data we hold about you
- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion** — request deletion of your TexoStat data (subscriptions, channels, connection logs)

To exercise these rights, email: **privacy@texflow.work**

## 7. Account Deletion

Deleting your TEX account will result in removal of your TexoStat subscription and channel data within 30 days. Connection logs are automatically purged after 30 days.

## 8. Security

Connection data is stored in JSON files on servers located in Australia. We use rate limiting and token-based authentication to prevent unauthorised access. No financial data is stored or transmitted.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes via the TEX platform. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

## 10. Contact

**Privacy enquiries:** privacy@texflow.work  
**Complaints:** You may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au if you believe your privacy rights have been breached.
