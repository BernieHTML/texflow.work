The test kit

The simplest way to prove LTEC works: pitch an insulating tent of metallic foils over the regolith to make your own shadow. The tent becomes the cold reservoir — so the compact engine runs almost anywhere, no permanently shadowed crater required.

2

3

4
Condenser inside the cold tent returns it to liquid.

Built and operated by astronauts or robots, the kit also powers nearby surface technology — a first demonstration before full PSR swarms. Geometry is illustrative; concept from the TexFlow LTEC paper, Section VI.
