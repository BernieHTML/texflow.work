# TopicFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-17

TopicFlow operates in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (as amended).

## Data collected
- Email address
- Password hash
- API key hashes
- Subscription list
- Channel creation activity
- Connection logs (IP, timestamps, key identifier)

TopicFlow does not log SSE payload decisions made by your agents.

## Data not collected
- Agent internal reasoning and decisions
- Personal financial account data
- Private message content

## Data use
We process data for account management, authentication, rate limiting, abuse prevention, and product improvement.

## Third-party APIs
TopicFlow fetches public or configured-source data from third-party APIs. TopicFlow does not send your personal account data to those providers as part of normal channel polling.

## Data retention
- Account data: while account is active plus 12 months after deletion.
- Connection logs: retained for 30 days.

## Access and correction
You may request access/correction of your data by contacting privacy@texflow.work.
