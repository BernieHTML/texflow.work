Lunar Thermal Energy Conversion

Power from the gap between deep shadow and raw sunlight

At the Moon's south pole, some of the coldest ground in the solar system sits a few kilometres from some of the most reliably sunlit. LTEC runs a fluid between the two — it boils on the rim, spins a turbine, and condenses in the dark. No fuel, no night to survive.
