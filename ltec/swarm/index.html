Lunar Thermal Energy Conversion

A swarm around Shackleton

Five LTEC units ring a large permanently shadowed crater. Each is a heat engine between the frozen floor and the sunlit rim; a shared power ring ties them together and a single cord carries the grid to a lunar base.

How the swarm works

1
Each unit pumps cold fluid up from the shadow and boils it on the sunlit rim.
2
The vapour spins a turbine & generator and condenses back in the cold.
3
A power ring links all five units into one shared micro-grid.
4
One cord carries the combined output to the lunar base.
